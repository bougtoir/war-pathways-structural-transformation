   timescale (~(K₀+S_p₀)/(b−ρ)); wars under scaling sponsors do not —
   observable in supply-vs-duration cross-conflict data.

   externally imposed depletion (because the latter is bypassable).

   threshold should show *lower* onset propensity at equal grievance —
