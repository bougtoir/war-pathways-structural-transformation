## Verdict: signature downgraded

The Phase-3 "hollow periods precede cessation" signature is produced by
*any* model containing either consent collapse OR position-dependent
efficacy — it is not a discriminating prediction for the lever.
Remaining discriminating content:

1. **Conditional exit dependence**: cessation probability increases with
   sponsor externality exposure specifically (e = c·ext), not with
   aggregate costs. Falsifiable if exit tracks generic attrition equally.
2. **Replenishment-law vs stock accounting**: the model predicts wars end
   when the *law* fails (sponsor exit) rather than when the *stock*
   visibly bottoms — a subtle but checkable timing prediction.

Rival explanations that would match the data instead: war economies
(Collier — hollow wars are profit, no legitimacy needed), learning models
(Slantchev — cessation from beliefs, not stocks), war-weariness
(weariness ∝ duration — produces hollow-looking ends without sponsors).
