# 00 — Phase 4 Executive Decision

**Decision: CONDITIONAL GO — bounded synthesis only. Phase 3's strongest
discriminating signature did NOT survive fair-complexity nulls.**

## What changed in Phase 4

1. **Gate 29 (reproduction):** all pivotal Phase-3 results reproduce
   bit-identically. Two corrections: (a) the finite-time bound's clock
   must start at *patron exit*, not onset — exit delays of 10–30 periods
   add 7–21 periods to observed spells; (b) hollow-period counts are
   threshold-sensitive (27–44 as threshold moves 0.1→0.4).
2. **Gate 30 (richer nulls):** a legitimacy-collapse-only rival produces
   16 hollow periods *without any sponsor mechanism* — the Phase-3
   "hollow-war timing signature" is not unique to the lever. What
   survives: only the *conditional exit dependence on sponsor
   externality*. Phase 3's discriminating claim is downgraded.
3. **Gate 33 (displacement as state):** the most damaging new negative
   result — when displaced violence persists 20–60 periods, the lever's
   total-harm advantage over baseline disappears entirely
   (0.057 ≈ 0.058) while violence periods explode 31 → 232.
4. **Gate 32:** sufficient conditions remain: exit guaranteed iff
   post-exit ρ < burn; stochastic replenishment erodes the guarantee
   (p_K_exit 1.0 → 0.93 at σ=0.025 with long exits). Impossibility
   region quantified, not claimed away.
5. **Gate 36:** at the illustrative 1,000T-JPY anchor, net value is
   negative in every tested cell — reported as sensitivity, not a
   feasibility verdict.

## Standing negative results (kept prominent)

- Channel A never terminates wars; hollow wars persist.
- Tolerant sponsors defeat the lever permanently (ρ ≥ burn).
- Barriers delay, never prevent (P(hit) → 1).
- Lever duration does not beat exogenous sanctions.
- NEW: displacement can erase all civilian-harm gains.
- NEW: hollow-war signature is not discriminating under fair nulls.

## Top-3 next research actions

1. Operationalize sponsor-externality tolerance from SIPRI/arms-transfer
   data — the one surviving discriminating variable (Gate 39).
2. Model displaced/proxy conflict as a coupled multi-actor process
   before any further lever claims — Gate 33 shows single-war metrics
   are misleading.
3. Decide whether the project continues as *synthesis formalization*
   (publishable contribution) or terminates the novelty pursuit — the
   mechanism-level novelty no longer survives fair comparisons.
