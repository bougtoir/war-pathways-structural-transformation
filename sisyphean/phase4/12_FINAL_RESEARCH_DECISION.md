# 12 — Final Research Decision

## CONDITIONAL GO — for a bounded synthesis, NOT a standalone theory

## What the theory is, exactly, after Phase 4

A formal synthesis of attrition, bargaining-termination, and
sponsor-exit mechanisms that adds two structural elements which survive
fair comparison:

1. **Stock-vs-law accounting**: separating depletion of a sustainment
   stock from endogenous change in its replenishment law, yielding a
   corrected finite-time bound (post-trigger clock) and a proven
   failure region (ρ ≥ burn).
2. **Conditional exit dependence**: cessation tracking sponsor
   externality exposure — the sole discriminating variable left after
   fair-complexity nulls absorbed the hollow-war signature.

## What was falsified or downgraded in Phase 4

- Hollow-war timing signature: not discriminating (legitimacy-only
  rivals produce it).
- Civilian-harm advantage: erased when displaced conflict persists —
  the single most damaging negative finding of the project.
- "Sisyphean Lever" as a name: a metaphor for a conditional mechanism,
  not a law; retained as project label only.

## Boundary of claims

- No prevention, no universal keystone, no real-world magnitudes,
  no advantage over exogenous sanctions, no guarantee under
  stochastic/unbounded replenishment, no validity under aggressor
  mis-identification, no empirical validation of Channel A.

## Top-3 next research actions (prioritized, ≤3)

1. **Sponsor-externality operationalization** — build the SIPRI/UCDP
   duration dataset and run the Gate-39 design: the only path from
   conditional theory to evidence.
2. **Coupled multi-actor displacement model** — before any further
   lever claims, model proxy/displaced conflict endogenously; Gate 33
   showed single-war accounting is wrong at the metric level.
3. **Synthesis write-up decision** — decide whether the accumulated
   formalization (gates, corrected bounds, failure-region theorems) is
   packaged as a synthesis contribution or the program's novelty
   pursuit terminates here. No further gate proliferation recommended.
