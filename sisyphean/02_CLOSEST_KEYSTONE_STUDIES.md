# 02 — Closest Studies to the Keystone Proposition

Gate 17 requires 5–10 works closest to the keystone claim, verified, with a
claim-by-claim overlap matrix. The Sisyphean Lever hypothesis is:

> **SL.** If aggressive-war sustainment requires a causally necessary enabling
> condition K (fiscal reserves + coalition support + materiel flow), and if
> war onset can endogenously raise the burn rate of K above its replenishment
> rate, then initiation itself degrades the conditions of continuation —
> without degrading the defender's sustainment and without using civilian
> deprivation as the mechanism.

## Claim-by-claim overlap matrix

C1: sustainment (not will) is the binding constraint on war duration.
C2: an identifiable, narrow enabling condition K exists (keystone).
C3: onset endogenously accelerates K-depletion (Sisyphean dynamics).
C4: mechanism does not require leader rationality or goodwill.
C5: aggressor/defender sustainment modeled asymmetrically (defense preserved).
C6: civilian welfare as hard constraint, not instrument.

**No single work satisfies C2+C3+C5+C6 jointly.** The lever is a *synthesis*
of C1 (sustainment binding, Powell/Slantchev), C3-flavored feedback
(Powell shifting power, Richardson), plus the new structural identification
C2 (a keystone stock with explicit burn/replenishment) and the normative
asymmetry C5–C6 (aggressor K depleted, defender sustainment and civilians
preserved). We classify the proposition as **synthesis with one new
structural element (C2 formalization)**, not as a discovery.

## Discriminating prediction (Gate 17 requirement)

If SL is more than relabeled attrition, it predicts: *wars end faster when
onset itself spikes K-burn than when the same cumulative burn is imposed
externally at constant rate* — i.e., onset-triggered depletion is
self-limiting even where externally imposed sanctions are bypassable, and
this asymmetry appears only when K-replenishment (sponsors, stockpiles,
import substitution) is bounded. See `07_GATE_REPORT.md` / simulation
`adversarial_failure`: when replenishment is unbounded, SL collapses into
ordinary attrition — which is exactly the counterexample we report.
