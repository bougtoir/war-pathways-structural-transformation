# 14 — Open Items and Terminology

## Terminology decision

"Sisyphean Lever" is kept as a provisional label (per the prompt's
instruction that it is a hypothesis label, not a novelty claim). In the
docs the mechanism is called **onset-triggered sustainment depletion**
when a descriptive name is needed.

## Open items for a future phase

1. **K operationalization**: map the abstract stock to measurable proxies
   (reserves, external supply tonnage, coalition commitments) and test the
   duration-ceiling prediction on conflict-duration data.
2. **Preemption/first-strike incentives**: explicit timing game where the
   barrier changes payoff ordering — currently flagged, unmodeled.
3. **Proxy substitution**: war displaced to a different dyad rather than to
   sub-state violence within the same dyad.
4. **Endogenous sponsor behavior**: sponsors deciding resupply
   strategically (currently ρ is exogenous and fixed).
5. **Institutional capture as a state variable**: capture should feed back
   onto both the barrier and K-regrowth, not just selection error.
6. **Empirical falsification of the discrimination prediction (2)**:
   onset-triggered vs externally imposed depletion requires conflict-level
   supply-flow data; not attempted here.
