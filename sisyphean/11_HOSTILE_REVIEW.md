# 11 — Hostile Self-Review

1. **"Your keystone is attrition with a new name."** Partially correct.
   What is new is only the *onset-triggered asymmetry* and the explicit
   stock-vs-flow accounting that makes the failure region (ρ ≥ b) precise.
   We claim synthesis, not discovery — see 02.
2. **"P(hit W)=1 everywhere — your barrier does nothing."** The barrier
   delays onset ~19 periods at B=3 and filters stochastic onsets 5–6×; it
   does not prevent war over long horizons. We state no near-zero claim.
   If the prompt's stronger proposition required prevention, it fails —
   reported as such.
3. **"Defender asymmetry is assumed, not derived."** True: defender
   sustainment is exogenously non-draining in the model. This is a
   modeling choice implementing the normative constraint, and it is
   labeled as such.
4. **"h_min=0.5 civilian constraint is arbitrary; you never bind it."**
   Under the lever h_final ≥ 0.91 — the constraint is slack *because* the
   mechanism is cheap for civilians. Under the forbidden comparator it
   binds hard (h→0). The contrast demonstrates the constraint works.
5. **"Stockpile/sponsor units are uncalibrated; numbers illustrative."**
   True and stated. This is theory development: parameter signs and
   ordering matter, magnitudes are illustrative.
6. **"Displacement is counted but not modeled."** Correct — displacement
   is a flagged Poisson reroute, not a second process. Residual gap.
7. **"eu rule dominates your onset economics anyway."** False in model:
   bureaucratic rule never uses utility; results hold.
8. **"First-strike incentives unmodeled."** Conceded — flagged, not
   resolved (gate 13).
9. **"Proxy warfare folded into sponsor is a dodge."** Partially: a
   sponsor bearing burn is exactly ρ↑; but proxy *substitution* (war
   elsewhere) is unmodeled — limitation.
10. **"Finite-lag exit is built in by K_min."** K_min is the operational
    necessity threshold, not the mechanism. The mechanism is b > ρ; where
    that fails, no exit occurs — shown empirically.
