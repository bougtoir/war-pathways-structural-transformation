# 14 — Civilian Preservation (Gate 13)

## What the mechanism protects — honest accounting

| dimension | protected? | mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| civilian lives | conditionally | harm h is a measured cost, never a success channel; collapse via devastation explicitly excluded |
| social infrastructure | partially | enters V(L) only as production substrate |
| economic production | partially | V depends on L — population welfare and coercer gain are both harmed by coercion; distinguishes "reduced coercer gain" from "improved civilian outcome" |
| political freedom | weakly | no freedom variable; L proxies cooperation freedom only |
| legitimate defensive capacity | yes in-model | d tracked, d ≥ d_min checked; balancer support endogenous |

## Distinction enforced

Reduced coercer gains ≠ improved civilian outcomes: the model tracks
both, and the mechanism's success metric (π∞<0) is defined on the
coercer side only. Nothing in the model rewards harm; nothing in the
model proves civilians are better off — harm is a *separate recorded
quantity* (harm_cum, h_final), and Phase-4's displacement finding
(widened violence) is preserved as the standing civilian risk.

## Verdict

CIVILIAN PRESERVATION: **CONDITIONAL GO** — constraints respected
(harm never credited, defense non-degraded in-model), but no theorem
or evidence shows civilian welfare *improves*; the claim is
non-degradation, not improvement.
