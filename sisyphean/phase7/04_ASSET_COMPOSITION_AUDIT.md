| category | exclusive? | identifiable? | problem |
|---|---|---|---|
| physical (w_phys) | mostly | yes (fixed capital share) | overlap with infrastructure serving networks |

enough for a 4-bin scheme, only partially empirically identifiable —
matches the Phase-6 empirical NO-GO.

## Endogeneity audit

Phase-6 declared asset shares "slow" but provided **no transition
equations**: w's are exogenous constants in every run. Claims about
investment/relocation/substitution are prose only — no conservation,
positivity, or stability to check because no dynamics exist.

- Conservation: not applicable (no transitions).
- Positivity: w ≥ 0 enforced by assignment only.
- Simplex: never imposed until this phase (doc 02).
- Stability: the two "stable regimes" of c6 are static parameters,
  not dynamical attractors — relabeled as such here.

## Verdict

The asset-composition machinery is **externally assigned, not
endogenous**. Phase-6 doc-04 overstated: slow-dynamics narrative
without equations. The simplest defensible transition (dw/dt =
investment − coercion-driven exit) is absent; adding it would be
expanding the theory, which this phase does not do.
