# 08 — Fair Rival Model Comparison (Gate 7)

Rivals built at comparable information/calibration: `verify7.py`
section (8), `results/r7_rivals.csv`.

## R1 — static appropriation (Grossman-Kim/Hirshleifer)

π_R1 = r·V̄ − c1r − c2r²: no state, no consent. At V̄=1, π=+0.60 — pays.
Difference: ours adds L-state; discriminating only in consent-
dominated region.

## R2 — dynamic hold-up (KCA)

Coercer extracts only physical quasi-rent: π_R2 = r·w_phys·V̄ − cost.
Profitable iff w_phys > (c1+c2r)/V̄. **Our model nests R2 exactly at
L=0 and converges to it as consent collapses** — the L-channel is the
only difference, i.e. the delta is behavioral, not structural.

## R3 — occupation cost (Liberman-style)

π_R3 = r·V̄ − c1r − (c2+occ)r², occ=0.15: still pays at V̄=1 (π=0.45).
Difference is a cost parameter — nothing discriminating.

## R4 — political survival

π_R4 = base + S_r (survival rent 0.3): coercion rational at ANY π<0 —
**rival explains persistence better than our model predicts cessation**
for regime actors. Fatal to rationality-independence, not to the level
theorem itself.

## R5 — combined strongest rival

R2 (physical extraction) + R4 (survival rent): coercion pays and
persists whenever survival rent positive — covers most of the
parameter space where our model claimed distinct behavior.

## Discriminating prediction — final accounting

Survives ONLY: at matched (V̄, c1, c2, r) and payoff-comparing actors,
abandonment probability covaries with the target's consent-dependent
asset share. Everything else is within R1–R5's joint span. Where the
behavioral spec is unknown, rivals and ours are observationally
equivalent — novelty is **conditional**, not robust.
