# 13 — Rationality Independence (Gate 12)

Required separation: economic unattractiveness vs behavioral deterrence
vs physical infeasibility vs institutional impossibility.

## Decomposition

- Economic unattractiveness: achieved (level condition) — narrow.
- Behavioral deterrence: only for payoff-comparing actors — narrower.
- Physical infeasibility: only via K-depletion (Phase-3 channel) — NOT
  the appropriability mechanism.
- Institutional impossibility: not modeled.

## Verdict

**Rationality-independence claim FAILS** wherever it appeared
implicitly. The repaired honest statement: the mechanism makes
coercion *economically unprofitable in a bounded region* — it deters
only actors who read payoffs. This was already the Phase-5/6 finding;
the independent audit confirms there is no path within the model to a
stronger claim, and no such claim may appear in any future framing.
