# 09 — Measurement Validity (Gate 8)

Central estimand: net appropriated value π = A − C over an occupation/
coercion episode, conditioned on the target's asset mix. Prohibited
substitutes (duration, incidence, external support) not used.

## Required quantities vs observability

| quantity | observable? | route | validity |
|---|---|---|---|
| extracted value A (fiscal/resource/enterprise/remittance channels) | partially | occupation budgets, reparations accounts, forced-trade balances | case-by-case; strong cases exist (Germany, Japan, Kuwait) |
| potential value V_pre | yes | pre-episode output series | standard |
| counterfactual V_cf | no direct | comparator dyads or synthetic control | contested, but specifiable |
| control costs C | partially | administration + suppression budgets | sparse outside major cases |
| net appropriation π | derived | A − C with deduplication | depends on channel coverage |
| asset mix (w_phys vs consent-share) | partially | sector value-added + skills structure | proxy-level, not direct |
| population exit / human-capital loss | partially | displacement + emigration records | partial |
| institutional/network disruption | weakly | no standard measure | weakest link |

## Units and periods

All flows in constant USD per occupation-year; stocks at control_date
boundaries; episodes censored at liberation/annexation-change dates.

## Missing data and selection bias

- Occupations with surviving fiscal records are *selected* — the
  well-documented cases skew to large organized occupiers.
- Destruction vs extraction inseparable where channels undocumented —
  coded "indeterminate," never imputed.
- Counterfactual contamination: sanctions/war co-occurring with
  occupation lower V_post without lowering A.

## Verdict

The estimand is **measurable in principle** for a bounded case series
via original coding (protocol exists, Phase 6), NOT measurable from
any assembled public dataset today — consistent with Phase 6 NO-GO.
