# 05 — Dynamic Self-Undermining Validation (Gate 5)

## Does coercion endogenously destroy its own effectiveness?

The model's feedback loop: r>0 → L↓ (population response) → V(L)↓ and
α(L)↓ → π↓ → (abandonment for some rules). PLUS K-depletion
(sustainment burn > replenishment, Phase-3/4 heritage).

## Six mechanisms kept distinct

| mechanism | in model? | interchangeable? |
|---|---|---|
| resource depletion (K) | yes — Phase-3 channel | NO — stocks, not response |
| declining economic returns | yes — via L→α,V | NO — behavioral, not stocks |
| loss of political objective | no | — |
| loss of cooperation | yes — the L channel | — |
| loss of mobilization | no | — |
| loss of external support | yes — patron exit | NO — exogenous tolerance |

## Honest verdict

Two independent exits exist in-model (unprofitable vs K-depletion) —
mechanisms not interchangeable and both needed. The coercion→L→π
feedback is a **genuine response-mediated loop** (not ordinary
depletion): it persists even with infinite K, which the depletion
model cannot produce — that is the model's only real mechanistic
distinction.

## Comparison to conventional resource-exhaustion

If K were removed entirely, L-collapse alone still ends coercion where
LVL binds (c3 grid: "unprofitable" exits exist). Conversely under a
physical asset mix with tolerant population, only K-depletion operates
— identical to ordinary attrition. **The novel mechanism exists but is
conditional on the consent channel being active** — in its absence the
model degenerates to textbook attrition, which is NOT a Sisyphean
mechanism by the program's own definition.
