## Proposed modest falsifiable design

Event-study / interrupted-time-series on war duration:

- Unit: intrastate+interstate conflicts (UCDP/COW dyadic-year).
- Treatment proxy: sponsor externality shock (e.g., patron domestic
  sanction/embargo event) → predicted ρ collapse.
- Prediction under lever: duration ≈ residual stock/burn; wars with
  *bounded* post-shock inflow end earlier. Under exogenous-sanctions
  null: same effect — so the discriminating test is the **timing-order
  prediction**: efficacy-collapse proxies (governance cooperation
  indices, diplomatic recognition withdrawal) should *precede* cessation
  in endogenous-channel cases but not in pure-attrition cases.
- Confounders: battlefield trends (control via COW battle deaths),
  selection (sponsors pick strong clients — instrument via patron
  domestic shocks), reverse causality (exit causes withdrawal — use
  leads/lags).
- Honest identifiability note: the keystone is only identifiable where
  inflow measurement is good; covert resupply makes ρ unobserved and the
  design uninformative — likely limits the test to well-instrumented
  cases.
