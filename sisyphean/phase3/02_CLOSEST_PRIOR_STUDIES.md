## Mechanism-level comparison

| work | self-undermining element | what Phase 3 adds |
|------|--------------------------|-------------------|
| Powell 2012 | fighting shifts future bargaining power — war erodes its own prize | separates V_G (efficacy) from U_L (will); hollow wars where V_G=0 but fighting persists |
| Powell 2006 | commitment problems: declining power fights now | sponsor endogeneity (patron tolerance to externality) as a replenishment-law channel |
| Slantchev 2004 | initiators learn + accumulate costs; termination by belief update | stock-based exit that needs no belief update (works under bureaucratic/intrinsic rules) |
| Fearon 2004 | sons-of-soil commitment problems lengthen wars | consent-holders as explicit actors withdrawing cooperation post-onset |
| Filson & Werner | sensitivity to ongoing costs | Channel A: battlefield success can be efficacy-hollow (consent starvation) |
| Wagner/Smith&Stam | fighting reveals information | Phase 3 does not need learning — works under no-belief rules |
| Lyall & Wilson | mechanization → insurgent recruitment: coercion is counterproductive | closest to Channel A; we formalize the withdrawn-cooperation variable and separate it from sustainment |
| Collier 2000 | quasi-criminal war economies profit from continuation | explains hollow persistence; we bound the persist-region by ρ≥burn |
| Collier & Hoeffler | feasibility > grievance | shared premise; Phase 3 adds onset-triggered feasibility reversal |
| Pape | denial beats punishment | consistent: our lever is a denial-type mechanism |
| Arreguín-Toft | weak actors win via strategy interaction | defender asymmetry made explicit; civilian coupling |
| Goemans | regime-survival extends war | implemented as g_type/regime rule — reproduces his mechanism as a special case (hollow persistence) |

**Closest single rival:** Lyall & Wilson (counterproductive coercion) and
Powell 2012 (eroding stakes). Neither formalizes (i) an endogenous
replenishment law switched by sponsor externality tolerance, nor (ii)
the V_G≠U_L separation producing *hollow war* as an observable state.
No full text found describing onset-triggered collapse of political
returns under a replenishment-law formalization — difference survives,
classified as synthesis+2 structural elements, not discovery.
