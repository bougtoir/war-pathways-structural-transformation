# 00 — Executive Decision (Phase 3)

**Decision: CONDITIONAL GO** (bounded theoretical promise; named missing proofs below)

## What survived Phase 3

1. A genuinely endogenous Channel B exists: onset changes the replenishment
   law ρ(t) (sponsor tolerance/exit/substitution), not just the stock K.
   When ρ < burn after sponsor exit, wars self-terminate on the
   finite-lag bound; when patrons tolerate externalities, they run to cap.
2. A genuinely endogenous Channel A exists but is weaker: consent-holder
   withdrawal drives V_G → 0 under both non-maximizing consent rules —
   yet **V_G collapse does not shorten war** (exit is sustainment-gated).
   Wars continue "hollow" 27–58 periods after V_G ≈ 0. This is an honest
   separation of goal failure from cessation, as the prompt required.
3. The discriminating prediction survives Gate 26: the endogenous model
   predicts V_G collapse *precedes* cessation while matched nulls
   (step-down, sanctions, attrition) produce cessation without any
   preceding efficacy collapse — a timing-order difference, plus
   conditional dependence of exit on sponsor tolerance that exogenous
   nulls lack. Duration alone does NOT discriminate (lever spell 56 vs
   step-down 56); we do not claim it does.
4. Hard constraints: lever is non-inferior to baseline on civilian harm
   (harm 0.020 vs 0.058) and defender capability; deprivation comparator
   catastrophically fails (h → 0). Protection is not assumed — H and D
   are dynamically coupled and degrade when protection is lowered.

## Missing proof (why conditional, not GO)

- Analytical bound on endogenous ρ(t) collapse is simulation-supported
  only; a closed-form patron-exit condition exists but the joint
  (consent × sponsor × defender) bound is numerical.
- Identifiability of the keystone in real conflict data unresolved
  (Gate 27 proposes a modest design; not executed).

## Name verdict

"Sisyphean Lever" is partially earned: onset does roll the stone back
downhill *for sustainment* when sponsors are withdrawable; for
political value it hollows the war without ending it. Keep as a bounded
metaphor, not a theory name. See 15_RESEARCH_DECISION.md.
