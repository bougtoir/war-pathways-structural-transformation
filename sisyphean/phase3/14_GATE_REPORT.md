## Discriminating predictions (final set)

1. **Timing order**: efficacy-collapse signals (recognition withdrawal,
   governance-cooperation decline) *precede* cessation in endogenous
   cases; attrition/sanctions/step-down predict no such ordering.
2. **Conditional exit**: war exit probability rises with sponsor
   externality exposure (observed patron tolerance binding), not merely
   with cumulative cost — distinct dependence, testable.
3. **Hollow-war signature**: conflicts continue measurably after
   goal-attainability collapse (V_G≈0) under intrinsic/regime goals —
   a state no matched null produces.
