## CONDITIONAL GO

## Final report answers

**(1) What is the keystone, concretely, and why causally necessary?**
Two coupled objects, not one: (a) the sustainment stock K whose positive
level is necessary for sustained intensity (will cannot substitute —
exit is unconditional below K_min); (b) its *replenishment law* ρ,
determined by sponsor tolerance to the war's externality. Necessity is
operationalized, not definitional.

**(2) What changes at initiation, who changes it, why endogenous?**
Onset raises observable intensity c, which (a) triggers consent-holder
withdrawal (L↓ → attainable efficacy V_G↓ for consent-dependent goals)
and (b) raises sponsor externality e=c·ext, eventually exiting patrons
(ρ↓ → K burn-out). Changed by third parties and sponsors acting under
their own non-maximizing rules — nothing writes "V_G falls at onset";
the off-arms (L_decay=0, tolerant patron) delete each channel.

**(3) Can war continue despite collapse of political purpose?**
Yes — the model's central honest result. Under intrinsic or
regime-survival goals, V_G floor holds and war persists; under
consent-dependent goals war runs "hollow" (V_G≈0, U_L>0) for tens of
periods until sustainment fails. Goal failure ≠ cessation.

**(4) What do sponsors, non-rational actors, and proxy displacement do?**
Sponsors bound the lever exactly: ρ≥burn → no exit ever (tolerant patron
counterexample proven). Non-rational rules still suffer the sustainment
channel but stretch spells and maximize displaced violence. Blocking
onset displaces violence sub-state — counted as cost.

**(5) Can civilians and defense be protected without assuming it?**
In-model yes: shorter wars cut harm ~3×, defender grows unharmed
(asymmetric sustainment + balancer support), h is dynamically coupled
not fixed. Protection fails when protection-level is degraded or in the
sponsor-rich failure region — reported, not assumed away.

**(6) What does it predict that war-termination/coercion models don't?**
The hollow-war signature (efficacy collapse *precedes* cessation),
conditional exit dependence on sponsor externality tolerance, and the
channel-separability result that value collapse cannot terminate wars
at all — only sustainment can.

**(7) Is 'Sisyphean Lever' earned?**
Partially: the stone rolls back on the *sustainment* slope
(conditional on bounded replenishment) but not on the *onset* slope —
onset is delayed, not prevented, and displaced upward violence flows
elsewhere. Keep as bounded metaphor; the scientific object is
"onset-triggered sustainment depletion with endogenous replenishment."

## Unclaimed (explicit)

- No prevention claims; no universal keystone; no real-world magnitudes;
- No claim the lever beats exogenous sanctions (it doesn't — R5);
- No resolution of preemption, proxy relocation beyond the sponsor
  channel, or ex-ante empirical identification of K.
