## Boundary of the rationality claim

- Sustainment mechanics (Channel B) work under every rule — including
  intrinsic-aggression actors who never evaluate utility: the keystone
  binds below the decision layer.
- **But**: non-maximizing actors keep harming indefinitely inside any
  single spell (spells remain ~50 under intrinsic/regime rules vs 31
  under eu — hollow persistence is real). The lever shortens their wars
  only via K-exit, not via their objectives.
- Displacement cost is *largest* under the least rational rules
  (blocked attempts reroute 21–40 events) — suppressing onset for an
  intrinsically violent actor displaces rather than eliminates violence.
- Claim restated honestly: rationality-independence holds for the
  sustainment channel only; onset frequency and per-spell harm remain
  rule-dependent; indefinite harm by actors indifferent to goal failure
  is a documented possibility, not ruled out.
