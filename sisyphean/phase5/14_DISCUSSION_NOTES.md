# 14 — Discussion Notes (interpretations, NOT demonstrated results)

Reserved speculation — excluded from theorems and empirics.

- **Conflict/peace transcendence**: if appropriability is asset-mix
  conditioned, the long-run deep structure may be the *composition* of
  social value — economies whose value is predominantly relational,
  consent-dependent, and networked may be intrinsically less
  coercible. Hypothesis, not result.
- **Plural compatible futures**: multiple non-conquest developmental
  paths could coexist if value-creation alternatives remain feasible;
  the model's UA term gestures at this only.
- **Aspirational communities crossing borders**: consent networks that
  do not coincide with territorial borders would make α fall with
  distance-to-network rather than to territory — speculation for a
  spatial extension.
- **Normative vs functional obsolescence**: abandoning war as a moral
  choice ≠ making coercion functionally useless. This program targets
  only the latter; the JME project's domain is the former. The two
  claims should never be conflated — a world that *cannot* profit from
  coercion is still free to be violent.
- **Self-undermining optimism trap**: every hopeful channel in this
  phase had a documented failure arm. The project's honest output is a
  map of where coercion keeps paying.
