# 08 — War as a Special Case (Gate 4)

War = coercive appropriation pursued at high r through mobilization m
and sustainment stock K — the same state variables, so the Phase-3/4
results carry over rather than being re-derived.

## Mapping

| general coercion object | war reading |
|---|---|
| onset (fires + feasibility + barrier) | bargaining failure → mobilization → initiation |
| consent collapse L | legitimacy erosion (Phase 3 V_G analogue) |
| sustainment K burn vs ρ | war economy & sponsor flows |
| patron exit on externality | sponsor withdrawal |
| hollow spell (A < 0.2·V0) | hollow war |
| proxy_left state | proxy/recurrence displacement |
| dur_max / unprofitable exits | termination |

## What lower appropriable value does NOT do

- **Not prevent aggression by non-maximizers.** intrinsic and
  regime_survival rules produce the longest coercion spells (100 and 89
  periods, g8_rules) — appropriability is invisible to them. Preserved
  from Phase 3–4 as the standing negative.
- **Not reduce destructive capacity.** A≠0 does not mean harm=0;
  inability to attain objectives ≠ inability to inflict violence
  (taxonomy §3).
- **Not out-perform generic cost.** Pure cost raises prevented onsets
  entirely (g5); the appropriability channel's claim is about realized
  value and sustainment, not deterrence.

## Constraints kept

Legitimate defense non-degraded (d_final ≥ d_min, balancer endogenous);
noncombatant harm is a cost counted separately (h_min gate).
Phase 4 stands as benchmark — every failure region it documented
(tolerant patrons, persistent displacement, mis-ID, stochastic ρ)
remains open; nothing here closes them.
