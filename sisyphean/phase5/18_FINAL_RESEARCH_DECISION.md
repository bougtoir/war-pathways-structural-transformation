# 18 — Final Research Decision (Phase 5)

## CONDITIONAL GO — bounded; one narrow testable contribution

The reorientation from A to B is conceptually sound and produced a real
(if small) formal object: the asset-mix-conditioned appropriability
decomposition. It also produced honest negatives: α is empirically
unmeasurable with public data; the mechanism adds nothing against
non-maximizing coercers; sponsor-rich settings remain permanent
failures; a generic cost increase out-performs the mechanism at
prevention.

## Single strongest insight

"Does conquest pay?" is the wrong question at the wrong resolution —
the answer is conditioned on the *asset composition* of the target.
Physical/industrial value is capturable (Liberman stands);
consent-dependent value is not — and the boundary between them is a
single parameter region (w_phys vs w_c) the model makes explicit.

## Best counterexample

Liberman's industrial-conquest arm, reproduced in-model
(physical_only: coercion pays, no unprofitable exits). Any theory of
structural non-appropriability must concede this domain.

## Most informative next test

The Phase-4/Phase-5 sponsor-externality duration design (Design B):
SIPRI exposure × patron-side shock on UCDP duration. It is the only
feasible empirical test of any surviving channel.

## Strict stopping rule

If Design B shows no sponsor-externality effect on duration (or
measurement remains impossible after original-coding assessment), the
program should terminate: the synthesis will have been fully mapped,
and no further gate proliferation is justified.
