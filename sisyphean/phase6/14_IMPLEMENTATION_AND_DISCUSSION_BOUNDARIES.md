## Feasibility separation (unchanged)

Technical feasibility ≠ social net benefit ≠ political financing ≠
long-run durability; none currently evaluates positive, and nothing in
the Phase-6 repair changes that.

## Boundary statement

Shared visions and border-crossing aspirational communities are
discussion interpretations (see Phase-5 doc 14) — never theorem
assumptions. Conversion and unemployment costs are never set to zero.
