# 08 — Measurement Model for B (Gate 6)

Post-occupation GDP alone is **rejected** as the appropriated-value
measure — it conflates production, destruction, and extraction.

## Decomposition required

| component | symbol | measurement target |
|---|---|---|
| pre-coercion potential | V_pre | output/income trajectories before r>0 |
| produced value under coercion | V_post | output under r, netting destruction |
| captured by coercer | A | fiscal transfers, resource rents, enterprise control, remittances, documented extraction |
| costs of control | C(r) | occupation administration + suppression budgets |
| civilian welfare | h | displacement, mortality excess, rights indices |
| counterfactual | V_cf | matched non-coerced comparators |

The estimand for the level condition is sign of π∞ = r·A − C over the
occupation horizon; the estimand for the *novel* prediction is whether
sign of π∞ covaries with the target's asset mix.

## Numerator / denominator / attribution problems (explicit)

- Numerator A: multi-channel (treasury transfers, forced trade balances,
  enterprise assets, reparations-in-kind) — components differently
  documented; summation double-counts when flows are gross.
- Denominator: per-period vs stock-total vs per-capita — must be fixed
  *a priori*; we use flow per occupation-year.
- Attribution: destruction vs extraction (both lower V_post; only the
  latter is A); baseline GDP under sanctions/war is contaminated —
  needs the V_cf comparator, not pre-war levels.
- "GDP of occupied territory" is an outcome of V_post, not of A —
  the earlier phase's temptation to use it is rejected.

## Source classes assessed

Occupation fiscal records (case archives), reparations accounts,
forced-trade balances, enterprise registries — documentary evidence
exists for a bounded case series (10_case matrix); no global panel
exists and none is pretended.
