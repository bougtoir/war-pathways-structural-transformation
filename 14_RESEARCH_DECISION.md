# 14 — Research Decision

## CONDITIONAL GO

The framework is promising but requires specific additional theoretical
work and cannot yet claim a novel contribution.

## Rationale

- **What survives:** an actor-split, gate-structured pathway model in
  which war requires the conjunction of willingness, feasibility, and
  escalation — so interventions at separate gates multiply. That
  integrated claim is defensible and we found no prior statement of it
  as a design principle (04, C3).
- **What does not survive unqualified:** "rationality-independence"
  (stage-3 still maximizes); "war obsolescence" (already established as
  a claim; the framework supplies mechanism, not thesis); unconditional
  stability (the evolutionary analysis says conditional — exploitation
  breaks it).
- **Why not GO:** every component is PARTIALLY ESTABLISHED; the
  independent contribution is integrative/diagnostic rather than a new
  theory; empirical measurability of the headline construct (|R|) is
  weak.
- **Why not NO-GO:** the decomposition produces non-obvious,
  discriminable predictions (stage-specificity of interventions;
  agency-gap wars; non-monotone connectivity) that existing accounts do
  not generate as cleanly.

## Required items (for GO/CONDITIONAL GO)

- **Single strongest theoretical contribution:**
  the conjunctive gate structure — willingness ≠ feasibility ≠
  escalation — with the leader-only war-rationality region as the
  measurable pathology it explains (wars the decider wants that the
  state's calculus rejects).
- **Most important falsifiable prediction:**
  opportunity-cost development reduces *mobilization success conditional
  on dispute onset* more strongly than it reduces *dispute onset itself*
  — the stage-specificity claim. If data show the opposite binding
  location, the architecture fails.
- **Strongest competing explanation:**
  commercial/capitalist peace (Gartzke 2007) — same dependent variable,
  correlational claim, no gate structure; plus selectorate theory for
  the actor-split. The framework must beat these on discriminating
  predictions, not generality.
- **Most appropriate future empirical test:**
  dispute-conditional analysis on UCDP/COW-MID merged with V-Dem
  accountability and SIPRI/WDI mobilization proxies: estimate which
  interventions predict onset vs. intensity vs. widening, testing
  stage-specificity.
- **Main unresolved theoretical weakness:**
  the selection stage still rests on expected-utility comparison — a
  maximization metaphor at the actor level — and the leader-only region
  is defined on subjective utilities that are only bound-identifiable.
  A non-maximizing selection stage (organizational/bureaucratic) would
  strengthen the rationality-independence claim but is not yet built.

## Conditions attached to the GO

1. Re-title claims: "independent of *state-level* rationality", not of
   rationality simpliciter.
2. Scope obsolescence claims to interstate organized mass violence with
   explicit violence-relocation tracking before any manuscript work.
3. Present |R| as a construct with bound-identification, never as a
   measured quantity.
4. Any further formal work must carry its own justification; the model
   stays minimal.
